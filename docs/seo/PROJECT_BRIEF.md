## Market approach
One English edition serves both markets. Each tool review shows UK (£, UK GDPR, HMRC MTD) and US ($, state privacy laws, sales tax, call-recording consent) notes side by side. Market-specific guides exist where the answer genuinely differs (UK: MTD, UK GDPR; US: bookkeeping/sales tax, US data privacy). hreflang lists en-GB, en-US and x-default pointing to the same URL.
