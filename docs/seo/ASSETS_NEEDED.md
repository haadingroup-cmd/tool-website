# Real images & details needed from the owner

The site currently uses generated SVG artwork (logo, guide covers, OG images). The prompt book (M01) asks for real or licensed images and no fake people/places. Please send:

1. **Logo** — HA Tools logo as SVG or a 1024×1024 PNG with transparent background (current one is a placeholder mark).
2. **Favicon/app icon** — square 512×512 PNG (can be cut from the logo).
3. **Editor photo + name + 2-line bio** — a real person responsible for reviews (strongest E-E-A-T signal). Plus LinkedIn URL.
4. **Tool screenshots** (your own, from your own accounts) — 1600×1000 PNG/WebP of the main screen, for: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft 365 Copilot, QuickBooks, Xero, HubSpot, Zapier, Canva, Fathom. More later.
5. **Social profile URLs** — LinkedIn page, X, YouTube (only real, active ones).
6. **Business facts** — legal/company name, country of registration, a working contact email on @hatools.boats.
7. **Optional:** a short screen-recording (30–60 s) of any tool workflow for a video section.

Do not send stock photos of "team" or "office" that are not you — those would be misleading.
